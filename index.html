How I
run the work.

Four steps. No mystery, no big reveal at the end.

01 (01)

Find the friction

I audit the current funnel, analytics, support tickets and session recordings. Most conversion problems are obvious once you look at where people actually stop — and it's rarely where the brief assumes.

02 (02)

Set the direction

One clear recommendation, not five options. I frame the problem, propose the approach, and get sign-off before pixels exist — so the build doesn't unravel later.

03 (03)

Prove it in Figma

Clickable prototypes, early and often. Real content, real edge cases — empty states, out of stock, long titles, failed payments. If it works in a prototype it survives build.

04 (04)

Hand over clean

Annotated specs, component states, responsive behaviour and motion notes. I stay on call through the build to answer questions, because that's where the last 10% lives.
