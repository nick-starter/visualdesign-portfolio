(04) — Method

How I work.

Four steps. No mystery, no big reveal at the end.

01 (01)

Find the friction

I audit the current funnel, analytics, support tickets and session recordings. Most conversion problems are obvious once you see where people actually stop.

02 (02)

Set the direction

One recommendation, not five options. I frame the problem and get sign-off before pixels exist, so the build doesn't unravel later.

03 (03)

Prove it in Figma

Clickable prototypes, early and often. Real content, real edge cases — empty states, out of stock, long titles, failed payments.

04 (04)

Hand over clean

Annotated specs, component states, responsive behaviour and motion notes. I stay on call through build, because that's where the last 10% lives.
