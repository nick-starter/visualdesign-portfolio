(00) — Design system

The style.

Every value on this site comes from five CSS custom properties and two type families. This page is the reference — what each token is, where it's used, and why.

(01) — Colour

Three colours.

--bg
Canvas #E3EAEC Page background. A cool grey, not white — keeps the pure-black type from vibrating and lets white cards read as raised.
--ink
Ink #000000 All body and display type. Not near-black — full black is the point, it's what makes the display type cut.
--red
Accent #FF0000 Pure red. Used once per viewport for emphasis — a number, a word, a section marker. Never for decoration, or it stops working.
surface
Surface #FFFFFF Cards, the project rail, nav, method steps. White on grey reads as elevation without a single shadow.
--muted #718584 --line rgba(0,0,0,.14) --line-strong rgba(0,0,0,.20)
(02) — Typography

Two voices.

Archivo Black shouts. The monospace explains. Every heading is the first, every label and paragraph is the second — that split is what gives the site its editorial feel without any ornament.

(05)

The rules.

What holds the system together when someone else is building in it.

(01)

One red per screen

The accent marks a single focal point per viewport. Two red elements and the eye stops knowing where to look.

(02)

Uppercase or nothing

Display type is always uppercase. Mono labels are always uppercase. Prose is never. No in-between.

(03)

Hairlines, not shadows

Separation comes from a 1px line and a change of surface. The only shadow on the site is the one under the nav.

(04)

Mobile is the default

Every clamp starts at its small value. The desktop size is the concession, not the baseline.
