(02) — Case study

Cargo
Crew

Uniform buying is a considered purchase — the wrong size or a missing logo means a return. I designed the product page, listing filters and bag-to-checkout path so the decisions a trade buyer needs to make are all answerable on one screen.

Client
Cargo Crew
Role
Product Designer
Year
2021
Scope
PDP · PLP · Cart · Checkout
(a)

The problem

The existing product page buried wholesale pricing, treated embroidery as an afterthought add-on, and pushed buyers through several steps to discover what their unit cost would actually be. Buyers were adding to cart to find out, then abandoning.

(b)

What I made

A product page that resolves size, quantity, logo method and unit cost in one place, with the customisation options positioned as part of the product rather than bolted on afterwards.

(c)

Outcome

  • 01Unit cost, including embroidery, visible before adding to cart.
  • 02Customisation treated as a product decision, not a post-purchase service.
  • 03Trade filters that match how buyers actually shop by role and volume.
