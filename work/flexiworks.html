(05) — Case study

Flexi
works

A trade buying flow for Officeworks, designed for a warehouse aisle rather than a desk. A tradie on a trolley with one free hand needs scan, add and adjust to take seconds — and needs to know their credit position before they fill it.

Client
Officeworks
Role
UI Designer
Year
2020
Scope
Cart · Scan · Credit
(a)

The environment

Everything about this flow is constrained by where it happens: a phone or tablet in one hand, a trolley in the other, poor connectivity, and a queue forming behind you. Desktop conventions actively work against that.

(b)

What I made

In-aisle barcode scan that adds to cart without leaving the shelf. Quantity steppers sized for a thumb. And a credit balance and activity statement that a trade account holder can check before committing, not after.

(c)

Outcome

  • 01Scan to cart without breaking stride in the aisle.
  • 02Controls sized and placed for one-handed use with a trolley.
  • 03Credit position visible before purchase, not discovered afterwards.
