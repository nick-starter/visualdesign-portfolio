(04) — Case study

Langton's

An account and CRM dashboard for a concierge sales team. This wasn't a consumer checkout problem — it was about giving a human seller enough context, fast enough, to make a relevant second suggestion.

Client
Langton's
Role
Product Designer
Year
2021
Scope
Account · Orders · Reporting
(a)

Designed for the seller

The customer barely touches this screen — a concierge seller does, dozens of times a day, while a buyer is on the phone. So the priorities were total orders at a glance, what to recommend next, and the reporting a manager actually asks for.

(b)

What I made

Recommendations surfaced above order history, because the seller's next move matters more than the seller's last one. Reporting split by varietal, region and channel so a manager could see where demand was actually coming from.

(c)

Outcome

  • 01Recommendations placed above history — the seller's next action, not the last one.
  • 02Sales splits by varietal, region and channel answered the questions managers were asking.
  • 03Built around lifetime value rather than a single transaction.
