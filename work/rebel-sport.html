(01) — Case study

Rebel
Sport

A full site redesign where I owned the interface from discovery research through to the specification a dev team builds from — homepage, listing pages, product pages and the promotional system, all mobile-first.

Client
Super Retail Group
Role
UI Designer
Year
2022 — 2023
Scope
Homepage · PLP · PDP · Promos
(a)

The brief

Rebel Sport needed a refreshed online store that could carry a wider assortment without losing the sport-led character the brand is known for — and a component set the internal team could keep shipping after I left.

(b)

Finding the friction

Before drawing anything I sat with analytics, session recordings and the support inbox. Three problems kept repeating: people couldn't find size or fit on a product page, promotional logic changed week to week so nothing was learnable, and the mobile product page carried desktop decisions.

(c)

What I made

Clickable prototypes, tested with real shoppers before anything was specced. Real content, real edge cases — sold-out sizes, long product names, split discounts.

(d)

Outcome

  • 01Concepts tested before build, so design churn during the development phase was avoided.
  • 02A promotional system the internal team could configure without design involvement.
  • 03Size and fit surfaced above the fold on every product page.
  • 04Component sheet handed over, letting the in-house team keep shipping after handover.
