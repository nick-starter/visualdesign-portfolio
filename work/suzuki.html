(03) — Case study

Build &
Order

A configure-to-order journey for motorcycles. The design problem wasn't styling — it was making a complex build feel like one decision at a time, with the price never disappearing and a single clear action to commit.

Client
Suzuki
Role
UX / UI Designer
Year
2021
Scope
Configure · Price · Dealer
(a)

The constraint

Buyers are on a dealer floor with a sales rep, or on a phone at home. Either way they need to trust the number. Ride-away price, finance estimate and total cost had to stay visible while they changed colour, added parts and chose a service package — and the build had to survive being half-finished and walked away from.

(b)

What I made

One step per screen, a persistent price summary that updates live, and a save-and-return model so a build isn't lost. Desktop and tablet first — this journey is rarely a phone-only task.

(c)

Outcome

  • 01Price never hidden — ride-away and finance estimate updated on every change.
  • 02One decision per step, so a build could be finished in a single dealer conversation.
  • 03Partial builds save and resume, so nobody lost configuration work by walking away.
  • 04One clear commit action, replacing several competing calls to action.
