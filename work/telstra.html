(06) — Case study

Recharge
Flows

Activation and prepaid recharge journeys for Telstra, plus contributions to the Able design system used across internal tools. High-volume, low-tolerance work — the flows have to be right because nobody is reading them twice.

Client
Telstra
Role
Visual Designer
Year
2020 — 2022
Scope
Activation · Recharge · Able DS
(a)

The constraint

People arrive here mid-task, often on a poor connection, often frustrated. The interface had to be unambiguous at a glance, recover gracefully from a dropped payment step, and work with the same components as the rest of the organisation.

(b)

What I made

Recharge and activation screens rebuilt on Able components so they inherited the organisation's accessibility and interaction standards rather than inventing their own — and so other teams could extend them without asking.

(c)

Outcome

  • 01Flows rebuilt on shared Able components, inheriting accessibility standards rather than duplicating them.
  • 02Payment failure states designed as a first-class path, not an error message.
  • 03Extendable by other teams without design involvement.
